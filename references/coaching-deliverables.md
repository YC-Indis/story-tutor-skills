## 3. Beat card and structure map

| Beat | Story function | Start → end state | New context/evidence | Question/pressure created | Physical/emotional line | Planned proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Review for:

- causal dependency instead of a list of events;
- only one controlling angle;
- context delivered before it is needed, not long before;
- escalation through cost, uncertainty, choice, or intimacy;
- a finale that pays the hook;
- reflection that is earned by what viewers experienced.

## 4. Script and voice map

| Section/beat | Core message or event | Evidence/visual | Audio source | Scripting level 1–4 | Unknown / field question | Approx. duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Voice-over necessity test:

1. What will the viewer fail to understand without this line?
2. Can action, reaction, production sound, or sequence communicate it better?
3. Does the line connect to visible evidence?
4. Does its opening receive the prior scene and its ending hand off the next?
5. Is it context, internal journey, compression, framing, or reflection?
6. What real-time moment must remain uncovered by narration?

## 5. Shot purpose and coverage plan

| Priority | Story beat | Viewer effect | Shot/coverage | Setting/character/camera choice | Audio | Risk/backup | Done test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Minimum scene coverage:

- geography/establishing;
- main action and consequence;
- reaction or decision;
- meaningful detail/insert;
- entrance, exit, or transition;
- sync sound, wild track, room tone;
- safety/consent and continuity notes.

## 7. Story / Engagement / Flow pass

Use separate passes rather than fixing everything at once.

| Time/range | Observation | Viewer effect | Factor | Proposed change | Preserve | Priority | Acceptance test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Pass order:

1. Context → Emotion → Rhythm.
2. Diversity → Authenticity → Originality.
3. Continuity → Eye trace → Aesthetic.

For each change, state whether it alters Selection, Chronology, Duration, or a downstream treatment.

## 9. Feedback brief and change list

Screening brief:

```markdown
Version and stage:
Film intention:
What is intentionally unfinished:
Questions for the viewer:
- What do you think the person wants?
- Where did you feel most/least invested?
- Where were you confused, ahead, or bored?
- What change do you think occurred?
- What do you remember afterward?
```

Evaluation table:

| Feedback item | Raw evidence | Reviewer type | Repeated? | Intention alignment | Root stage | Decision | Change/test | Acceptance test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Decisions: Accept / Test / Decline. Record why. Do not turn every proposed solution into an edit.

## 10. Timestamped review priorities

- **P0 — structural:** promise, causality, missing task/conflict/change, major factual or ethical failure.
- **P1 — story:** missing context/emotion, wrong chronology, damaging duration, weak scene change.
- **P2 — engagement/flow:** habituation, authenticity, continuity, eye trace, transition, clarity.
- **P3 — polish:** shot repair, graphics, subtitles, sound texture, color, delivery detail.

P0/P1 normally block downstream polish. Report only issues that materially affect the viewing experience; do not manufacture a fixed count.

## 11. Seven-day edit sprint

```text
Day 1: Intention + organization + technical preflight
Day 2: Watch, sync, selects, gap/risk map
Day 3: Full assembly and story spine
Day 4: Rough scenes and boundary pass
Day 5: Feedback and fine-cut timing lock
Day 6: Picture + sound + color by priority
Day 7: Export QC + archive + evidence book
```

Scale the workload, not the dependency order. For a complex film, expand days into weeks.

## 12. Project scorecard

Score only with evidence. A suggested 100-point distribution:

| Dimension | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Story clarity and change | 25 | First-time viewer retell; task/conflict/transformation |
| Emotion and authenticity | 15 | Viewer reaction; credible moments and sound |
| Rhythm and engagement | 15 | Attention notes; section-level style curve |
| Continuity and eye trace | 10 | Flow sweeps; unintended distractions |
| Picture, graphics, subtitles | 10 | Picture-lock QC |
| Voice, sound world, mix | 10 | Translation tests and audio-lock QC |
| Color and visual coherence | 5 | Scene match and final QC |
| Delivery and recoverability | 5 | Render review, manifest, restore test |
| Process evidence and learning | 5 | Versions, decisions, next targets |

Do not let a strong polish score compensate for a broken story.
